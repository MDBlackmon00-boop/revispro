1. Who we are

Revis Pro LLC is a Wyoming LLC. We operate Revis Pro, a B2B sales CRM for business users. Product access and payments are hosted via Whop (revispro.whop.site and whop.com/revispro/revispro-app). 2. Information we collect

Account and contact information

When you buy or access Revis Pro through Whop, Whop may collect account details such as name, email address, payment method information, and membership status. We may receive related account or contact details needed to provide support and the Service. If you email us at contact@revispro.com, we receive the content of your message and your email address.

Customer data in the CRM

If you use the product, you may enter business sales data (for example contacts, deals, pipeline notes). That data is processed to operate the CRM for your account.

This static marketing site

The revispro.com one-pager is a static marketing site. As of the effective date, it does not load third-party advertising pixels or analytics trackers that we intentionally install. Your browser may still send standard technical data to our hosting provider (such as IP address, user agent, and request logs) when you fetch pages. We do not invent or claim additional trackers on this site. If we add cookies or analytics later, we will update this Policy.

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Revis Pro CRM;

  • Protect security, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

4. Sharing

We share information only as needed to run the business:

We do not sell personal information.

We retain account, support, and CRM data for as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Whop may retain payment and membership records under its own policies.

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the nature of a small B2B SaaS business. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use strong credentials and protect access to your Whop membership.

The Service is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (or under 16 where that is the applicable age). If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact contact@revispro.com and we will take appropriate steps.
